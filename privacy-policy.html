Privacy Policy

Word Wonders — Word Puzzle Game

Effective date: 25 July 2026  •  Last updated: 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Word Wonders ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is developed and published by Nivrutti Aware ("the Developer"). By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and the Developer does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

Game data — your current level, coin balance, bonus words, and sound, music and vibration preferences — is stored only on your device in local app storage. It is never uploaded to us and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, including:

  • your device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate (country/region-level) location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version, language, and network type;
  • ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks, and views.

This data may be used to deliver personalised ads where permitted, to limit repeated ads, to measure ad performance, and to detect invalid activity and fraud.

You can control or reset your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalisation, at any time on your device under Settings → Google → Ads.

For details on how Google uses this information, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy

3. Permissions

The App requests only the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — required to load advertisements. The game itself is fully playable offline.
  • VIBRATE — used solely for haptic feedback during gameplay. Vibration can be switched off in the App's Settings.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, SMS, call logs, or precise location.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

5. Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data, because we do not collect any. Advertising-related data described in Section 2 is collected and retained directly by Google under its own privacy policy and retention practices; the Developer receives only aggregated, anonymous performance reports (for example, total impressions and revenue).

6. Security

Because no personal data is transmitted to or stored by the Developer, there is no personal data on our side that could be exposed in a breach. Data held on your device is protected by your device's own operating-system security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California), or India's DPDP Act — including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. As the Developer holds no personal data about you, such requests relating to advertising data should be directed to Google. You can remove all data the App holds by uninstalling it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
